# Work-focused application

The application uses the Office layout. Manual emotes, the telescope, seasonal themes and costumes, cars and their scenic route, the office dog, Minesweeper and BLOCKFALL, the jukebox, coffee and smoking have been removed from the client, server, protocol, settings and dedicated assets.

Stairs and fire poles, weather and day/night, celebrations, ordinary avatars and office collaboration tools remain. The rooftop venue, its elevator access, bar, DJ, drinks and rooftop activities remain available while its replacement meeting room is designed separately. The city backdrop remains, without decorative car traffic.

The fire poles use a dedicated dust renderer. The rooftop DJ retains its own music bus, volume and shared server clock. The desktop books remain in the shared Blender models. The office kitchen, its appliances and their audio have been removed. Blender source scripts match these assets.

After updating the server, reload open browsers to receive the matching bundle. Obsolete WebSocket commands are ignored. Old theme, dog, jukebox and arcade data files are no longer read; updating does not delete project data, accounts or credentials. Positions saved outside the remaining grounds return through the elevator; profiles and useful preferences remain. Only the obsolete browser lap score is removed alongside the already-retired golf score.

The new rooftop meeting-room model is a separate change. Importing that model will require dedicated seating/session/whiteboard integration; this cleanup does not alter the existing rooftop interior or install agent software.
